Which products sellbelow their true cost?

In 30 seconds
Pain

Sales were going up; margin was not.

What I did

I computed the true cost with fees, shipping and returns.

Insight

Seven products were sold below their true cost.

Tools

BigQuery · SQL · Power BI · DAX

Data

Simulated, with real problems: duplicates and missing costs.

01The pain

Selling more was not leaving more.

In three months sales grew 18%, but margin stayed flat. Nobody knew which products were eating it.

02What I built

Out of every 100 sold, 17 remain.

A waterfall per product with every cost that is not on the invoice. It reads left to right, without explanation.

03The recommendation

Adjust four prices and drop three products.

The question changes from “how do we sell more?” to “what is worth selling?”.

7

products sold below their cost

04Technical detail

Star schema and one measure per cost.

Sales and cost facts at line level, product, channel and date dimensions. Costs without an invoice are allocated by rule.

True margin = VAR sales = [Net sales] VAR costs = [Product cost] + [Commission] + [Allocated shipping] + [Returns] RETURN DIVIDE ( sales - costs, sales )
