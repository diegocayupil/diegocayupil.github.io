00Signal or noise

Before explaining a change,I check that it is one.

Every metric goes up and down by itself. I explain what leaves its normal variation.

✗Noise93.0%: inside the normal range.

Looking for a cause means inventing one. Next week it comes back by itself.

✓Signal92.4%: outside the normal range.

Something changed in week 42. This one gets analysed.

Where the band comes from: from the metric’s own history. Average ± 2.66 times the average change from one week to the next.

Example figures. The method is Wheeler’s, “Understanding Variation”.

00Six traps

Six trapsand how I avoid them.

An average

One number for everyone

✗

Split by segment

The total can fall while every segment rises: the mix changed.
Two lines that rise together

“One causes the other”

✗

A group without the change

Moving together isn’t causing. I compare with those who didn’t get the change.
A small base

“It doubled”

✗

With how many cases

Going from 2 to 4 is +100%. I say how many cases are behind it.
Who is in the data

Only those who stayed

✗

Those who left, too

A customer who left doesn’t answer the survey. I look for who is missing.
The worst of the month

“The plan worked”

✗

Against its own history

After an extreme month, the next one is usually closer to normal. With or without a plan.
December against November

One month against the last

✗

The same month last year

Seasons repeat. I compare each period with its equivalent.
