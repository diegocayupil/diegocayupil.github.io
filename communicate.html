00The chart

The question picks the chart.Not taste.

Seven questions cover almost everything a dashboard gets asked.

The question→What it compares→The chart
“How much, against the target?”
It’sMagnitude

✓Bars from zero, with the target as a line.

“How did it change?”
It’sChange over time

✓A line. Bars only for a few periods.

“Who comes first?”
It’sRanking

✓Horizontal bars, sorted.

“What share is it?”
It’sPart of a whole

✓One stacked bar. A pie only with two or three parts.

“How is it spread?”
It’sDistribution

✓A histogram. The average alone hides it.

“Do they move together?”
It’sCorrelation

✓A scatter plot, saying what each dot is.

“What is the exact figure?”
It’sLookup

✓A table. Not everything is a chart.
