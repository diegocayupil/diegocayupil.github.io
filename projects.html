Case 01 · Operations · simulated data

Fill rate to the chains fell from 95% to 88%. Where is it lost?

  2. Part 1 · Starting from zero
  3. Part 2 · The drop
The case, before step 1

Four chains, one number nobody measured.

Who
Despensa Quila, a (fictional) grocery maker that ships to four supermarket chains.
The ask
“Each chain complains with its own number. I need one number for all four.”
What’s at stake
The agreements with the chains require a 95% fill rate. Months later, it drops to 88%.
● Live · try it

One number for all four chains.

  1. 1✗ The problemEach chain complained with its own number: the same quarter came out at 95%, 90% or 59%.
  2. 2What I didI agreed what delivering means: what the chain receives in full and on time, in units.
  3. 3✓ The resultOne definition and one target: fill rate in units, 95%.
