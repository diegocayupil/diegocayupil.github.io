    00Saying no

    Saying “this isn’t a dashboard”builds the most trust.

    I don’t sell what I know: I solve what’s there.

    When the request is a question answered once, I say no to the dashboard and yes to the answer.

    A managerBuild me a dashboard to see why sales fell in March.
    1I name what it isWhat you describe is an analysis, and I can do it.
    2I say why notA dashboard is for looking at the same thing every week. This gets answered once.
    3I propose, with a dateIn one week I hand you the written conclusion.
    4I leave the door openIf something shows up to watch every week, that’s the dashboard.
    ✗If I say yes

    How long6 weeks of work

    What they getA dashboard opened once

    What remainsThe question stays unanswered

    ✓If I say no

    How long1 week

    What they get2 pages with the answer

    What remainsNext time they call me first

    They win: an answer, and sooner. I win: next time they ask me before deciding what to ask for.
